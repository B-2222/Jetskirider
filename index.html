        MONEY, SKIS AND MAPS

        • Cash: every trick pays (bigger tricks and combos pay more). So do buoy races, missions, and spraying other riders online with your jet. Your cash and skis are saved on this device.
        • Garage: you start on the Spark Trixx. Pick a locked ski to see it, then BUY it once you have the cash.
        • TRIM-X: a kit for each sit-down ski. Stage 1 adds trim range for a proper rooster tail; Stage 2 trims even further for a massive one. Trim up with E.
        • Maps: Palm Bay, Thunder Reef (giant fast surf), Glass Lake (flat water and quick rollers) and Storm Coast (rough, rain, heavy surf). Each one has its own cash bonus.
        • Buoy race: ride into the glowing race circle (follow the green RACE arrow at the top, or pick Race circle in the garage) and stay in it for 10 seconds. Anyone in the circle with you races you. Everyone goes to the start line, then 3, 2, 1, GO: cross the start line, take every gate in order and come back to the finish. Follow the green arrow over your ski. Faster times win better medals, and beating mates pays extra.
        • Account: SIGN UP in the garage with your email and a password and your progress saves to your account. Log in on any device to carry on.

        TRICKS

        • Stand-ups: no trim; lean the whole ski into turns, hold Shift to slide. In the air: Space backflip, steer to spin. Space starts the flip the moment you are in the air: on a small jump you may not get round, so pick a decent wave. Hold Space up the face and through the lip for the biggest flips. Let go once the rotation is under way and the rider lands it.
        • Flat-water backflip (stand-ups): at low speed hold Shift without steering to sink the nose, then hit Space: the nose pops out of the water. Keep holding Space to flip.
        • Superman (sit-downs): off a wave jump press X right after take-off: the rider hangs on to the bars and kicks their legs out behind the ski. Let go before you land or you bail. Combine it with a lip spin for a SUPERMAN + 360.
        • Carve: just steer. The ski stays hooked up, banks over and throws a fan of water off the outside.
        • Powerslide: hold Shift while you turn. The rider throws the hull onto its side, the V bottom comes out and it lets go. Big skis take longer to tip. Let go of Shift to hook back up.
        • 180: stay on full lock through the slide. When you are going backwards with the nose down, hit the throttle and the pump fires a column of water.
        • Throttle hop: trim up, then stab the gas. The bow snaps up and the ski jumps clear of the water.
        • Wheelie: trim all the way up, pull back, gas.
        • Wave zone: proper surf: waves come through in sets with calm gaps between, each with a peak and shoulders, and they break, peeling out from the peak with a throwing lip. Hit the faces before they break to launch, or ride along the face under the curl for a barrel. To spin, throw the ski into a hard turn right at the lip: whatever rotation you take off with carries through the air. In the air Space and Shift only nudge the nose up or down (and X throws a Superman). Land straight for a clean-landing bonus.
        • Air: hit a ramp or a wave face. Shift your weight with Space / Shift to land nose-level.

        Gamepad: left stick steer and lean, RT gas, LT brake, bumpers trim.
